About Us

Built on commitment,
crafted with care.

ARMA Real Estate Ltd. is a disciplined firm engaged in real estate and property development, with strong working knowledge in development and construction. Formed in 2006, the company has continued to grow in size and diversity — successfully delivering commercial and residential projects across Dhaka.

We have an enduring commitment to quality and apartment owner satisfaction. Your dream, combined with our expertise, creates the balance between plan and delivery. Every apartment is built with detailed attention that is second to none — ready-to-move apartments, independent villas, penthouses, and luxury residences suited to budget and taste.

Our Mission & Values

Mission, Vision & Values

What guides every ARMA Real Estate project — from first drawing to handover.

Our Mission

Providing benefits to all of our business associates, attaining best management practices, and becoming a world-class company in the segments we serve remains our foremost mission.

  • Empowering customers with technologically advanced products at competitive rates — the best value for their money.
  • Organic growth through visionary leadership and teamwork across people, processes, products, and services.
  • A work culture that fosters individual growth, team spirit, and creativity.
  • Integrity, honesty, and internationally acceptable ethical standards.

Our Vision

To feature among the top companies of Bangladesh in the segments we serve — and to make the world a better place to live by developing products aligned with our commitments to the environment and energy.

Our Strength

  • Practicing ethical standards
  • Positive attitude & transparency
  • People’s participation & empowerment
  • Culture building and value enrichment

Commitment

We create value across the real estate market by focusing on service excellence and quality — for corporate clients and individual customers alike.

Integrity

We display integrity in every business decision and professional relationship, supported by open communication and teamwork.

Responsibility

We take seriously our role as a corporate body — enhancing profitability while serving stakeholders, community, and the environment.

Safety

Safety is a core value over which no business objective can have a higher priority.

Chairman’s Message

Bangladesh stands at a stage of take-off. Rapid growth across the economy continues to draw global attention. Power, real estate, and agriculture are among the sectors experiencing the most significant change — and at ARMA GROUP we are determined to play a meaningful role in their development.

We work toward a strong foundation and a professional identity of our own. In real estate, agriculture, and power, professionalism is the key word for success. We are committed to high standards in quality control and workmanship. Our philosophy is simple: give the customer value for his money.

Our people are trained and motivated; our financial fundamentals are strong. We will continue to expand and diversify — a progressive company playing a dynamic role in Bangladesh’s economic development.

Abdur Razzaque
Chairman, ARMA GROUP
