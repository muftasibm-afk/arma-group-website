Certificates & test reports

Each document opens to its full scan alongside what it verifies, who issued it, the accreditation behind that issuer, and the figures as printed.

On dates and standing. Certification terms are shown exactly as printed, including expiry. Where a term has ended it is labelled, and the ISO 9001:2008 certificate is against a revision superseded by ISO 9001:2015 — it stands as evidence of a long-running quality programme at the plant, not as a certification currently in force. Material test reports do not expire: they certify the samples tested on the date tested, and remain valid evidence of what the steel did.
