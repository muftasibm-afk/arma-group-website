CERTIFICATES & TEST REPORTS

Each document opens to its full scan alongside what it verifies, who issued it, the accreditation chain behind that issuer, and the figures exactly as printed.

On dates and standing. Certification and membership terms are reproduced exactly as printed, expiry dates included, and any term that has ended is labelled as such. Material test reports are different in kind: they certify the samples tested on the date tested and carry no expiry, so they remain valid evidence of material performance regardless of age.
